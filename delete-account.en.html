How to delete your Yesterday Wardrobe account

Use this page if you cannot open the app, or if you need the off-app deletion entry required by Google Play. Developer / operator: Emma Kao (brand / app: Yesterday Wardrobe, Yestora).

Option 1: Delete in the app (recommended)

This clears the local wardrobe, preferences, and sign-in state.

  1. Open Yesterday Wardrobe and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Choose “Delete account and all data” and confirm.

Option 2: Email us (off-app)

If you cannot use the app, email us to request deletion. Use the same email as your sign-in account, and put “Delete account” in the subject.

Email us to delete your account

Or email: yestora.app@gmail.com

Include: the email you sign in with, sign-in method (Google / Apple), and confirmation that you want to delete the account and related data.

What happens after deletion

See the full Privacy Policy for details.

  • We will reasonably delete or de-identify server-side data related to your account that we control. We will not merely freeze the account.
  • For legal duties, disputes, fraud prevention, or security investigations, we may briefly retain some audit / transaction records; after that period we delete or de-identify them.
  • Wardrobe photos that exist only on your device must be removed by deleting in the app, clearing app data, or uninstalling.
  • Data held by Google / Apple / RevenueCat must be handled through their processes. Cancel store subscriptions in Google Play / App Store.
